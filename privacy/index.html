Privacy policy

Short version: your rehearsals stay on your iPhone. Stepback has no account, no ads and no analytics.

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The scores you give yourself, the time each rehearsal took and the tasks you unticked.
  • The items you ticked in the kit list.
  • Your settings: exam, voice, speaking speed, Spanish, and any section time you changed.

All of it is stored in the app's own storage on the device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

The proctor's voice

The voice is produced on the device by the speech synthesizer built into iOS. Stepback does not use the microphone, does not record anything and does not send text to a server to be spoken.

Purchases

Stepback Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your email or your payment details. To know whether Pro is active and to count sales, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random identifier created for the app and the record of the purchase (product, date, price, country of the store). That record is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or sites. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What Stepback does not do

  • No account, no sign in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

Stepback is made for adults and students preparing a professional licensing exam. It is not directed at children.
